About

DengueSense QC is a public-health decision-support prototype for Quezon City. It combines the CPDD district maps / PSA 2020 Census demographic reference with weekly case, rainfall, and environmental data to project short-term dengue trends, run Bayesian risk inference, and generate experimental LSTM forecasts across the city's 6 districts and 142 barangays.

Barangay-level demographic figures are authoritative reference data. Current cases, rainfall, forecasts, alerts, and risk labels remain research/demonstration outputs and are not intended for operational public-health decisions.
